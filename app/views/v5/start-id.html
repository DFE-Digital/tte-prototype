Check if you're eligible for funding

You can check if you’re eligible for funding before you register. You do not need to sign in to do this.

If you’re not eligible for funding, you can choose to pay for the course another way. This is also known as self-funding.

{{ govukButton({ text: "Check eligibility for funding", href: "route-course", isStartButton: true }) }}

Register for a professional development course

If you have already checked your eligibility and have come back to register you'll need to re-answer the eligibility questions.

To register, you need to sign in with GOV.UK One Login – you can create an account if you do not already have one.

You'll need:

  • a mobile phone to receive a security code

You may also need:

  • your physical passport to prove your identity
  • your National Insurance number or teacher reference number (TRN)

By continuing, you agree to our privacy notice.
