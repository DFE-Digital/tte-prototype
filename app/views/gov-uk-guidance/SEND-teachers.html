A course to help experienced teachers deepen their understanding of effective practice related to inclusive learning. Using the latest research and enhanced strategies to support learners with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) relevant to the current classroom.

Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • teachers who want to improve their knowledge and skills in supporting and meeting needs in their setting

Overview

This course is from the {{ data['tte-programme-name-with-acronym'] }} suite of courses.

This course will help you to:

  • develop your understanding of high-quality teaching practices and inclusive approaches to supporting learning
  • understand strategies and approaches to support learning outcomes for children and young people who may have SEND

How the course will be delivered

This course will:

  • take approximately 2 school terms to complete (minimum 20 hours)
  • be a mix of self-study and live online training
  • start in October 2027 or early 2028 (January or February), depending on the training provider

Funding eligibility

Funded places are available for most state-funded settings, including academy trusts.

Examples of places that are not eligible for funding include private schools and schools outside of England.

If you are self-funding, you still need to register with the DfE.

When you register with the DfE, we will tell you if you are eligible for funding.

Course content

Inclusive practice

You'll learn how high-quality education can have a positive impact on outcomes through:

  • understanding how a culture of inclusion and belonging enables good outcomes
  • strengthened awareness of high-quality effective teaching
  • inclusive routines and environments

Understanding and supporting children and young people with SEND

You'll learn how to understand and support differences in learning, including through:

  • identifying needs and barriers to learning
  • understanding differences in learning
  • working with parents and families

Additional support

You'll learn how to utilise different approaches to supporting learning barriers, including through:

  • effective targeted interventions
  • working with teaching assistants and support staff
  • enabling positive transitions for pupils and learners
