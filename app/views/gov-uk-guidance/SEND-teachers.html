Guidance

Supporting SEND and inclusion (teachers) Experienced Teachers and Leaders Training (ETLT) course

A course to help teachers learn about effective pedagogy and practice relating to inclusive teaching and learning environments and strategies and approaches to support learners with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND).

Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • teachers who want to improve their knowledge and skills in supporting and meeting needs in the classroom
  • teachers who have already completed their induction

Overview

This course is from the Experienced Teachers and Leaders Training (ETLT) suite of courses.

This two-term course will help you to:

  • develop your understanding of high-quality teaching practices and inclusive approaches to supporting learning
  • understand strategies and approaches to support learning outcomes for children and young people who may have SEND

This course will:

  • take 2 terms to complete
  • involve up to 1 hour of study each week
  • be a mix of self-study and live training
  • start Autumn 2027

Funding

Funding to cover the course fees is available for most teachers and school leaders in state funded settings in England.

Course content

Inclusive practice

You'll learn how high-quality education can have a positive impact on outcomes through:

  • understanding how a culture of inclusion and belonging enables good outcomes
  • strengthened awareness of high-quality effective teaching
  • inclusive routines and environments

Understanding and supporting children and young people with SEND

You'll learn how to understand and support differences in learning, including through:

  • identifying needs and barriers to learning
  • understanding differences in learning
  • working with parents and families

Additional support

You'll learn how to utilise different approaches to supporting learning barriers, including through:

  • effective targeted interventions
  • working with teaching assistants and support staff
  • enabling positive transitions for pupils and learners

Course structure

The course includes 9 topics, outlined in the course framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

The course includes:

  • independent study
  • group coaching
  • virtual sessions with other participants

You’ll need to spend up to 1 hour a week working on this ETLT course.

If you can, you should do this during work hours in career development time.

Providers

You can train for this ETLT course with one of the following providers:

  • Ambition Institute
  • National Society for Education
  • Education Development Trust
  • LLSE
  • National Institute of Teaching
  • Teach First
  • University College London (UCL) Institute of Education

They work with delivery partners, such as teaching school hubs, to provide training.
