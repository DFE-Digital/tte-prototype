1. Choose a NCEP course

Find out about the different NCEP courses.

If you’re employed, talk to your employer. You may need their approval to take an NCEP course. They may be able to help you choose a course that’s suitable for you and your workplace.

Some courses have specific eligibility requirements, so check you’re eligible before you register.

2. Check if you can get funding

Most teachers and school leaders in state funded settings in England can get funding for NCEPs to cover the course fees.

3. Find a training provider

If you’re employed, ask your workplace if they already work with a particular training provider.

You can also contact providers directly. Refer to the course pages to find which providers offer your course:

  • Teaching in reception course

Alternatively, find your local teaching school hub and ask them who provides your chosen NCEP in your area.

Most providers operate throughout England, and your NCEP course will not include any face-to-face sessions that you will be required to attend in person.

4. Register with DfE

After selecting your course and identifying a provider, you must first register for your NCEP course with the DfE. Once registered, you can then apply through your chosen provider.

This is so that DfE can keep your professional record up to date, check and allocate NCEP funding, and issue NCEP certificates.

Even if you’re not funded, you still need to register.

5. Apply through your provider

As well as registering with DfE, you also need to apply directly to your provider.

Your provider will send you an application form by email once you’ve registered with DfE. They will assess your suitability for the course. If your application is successful, they will confirm how your course will be funded.

6. Start your NCEP course

Once you’ve applied, your provider will confirm whether you have a place on the course and will give you your course start date if you’ve been accepted.

If you complete your NCEP course, you can access your teaching qualifications to get your certificate.
