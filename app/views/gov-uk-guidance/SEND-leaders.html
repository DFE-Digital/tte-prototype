Guidance

Leading SEND and inclusion (leaders) Experienced Teachers and Leaders Training (ETLT) course

A course to help leaders learn about effective leadership and practice relating to inclusive school and college environments and supporting learners with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) in your setting.

Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • school leaders

Overview

This course is from the Experienced Teachers and Leaders Training (ETLT) suite of courses.

This three-term course will help you to:

  • develop your understanding of the benefits of inclusive practice on children and young people's outcomes and how to foster a culture of belonging and engagement in your setting
  • understand strategies and approaches to support learning outcomes for children and young people who may have SEND across your setting

This course will:

  • take 3 terms to complete
  • involve up to 1 hour of study each week
  • be a mix of self-study and live training
  • start Autumn 2027

Funding

Funding to cover the course fees is available for most school leaders in state funded settings in England.

Course content

Inclusive culture and environments

You'll learn how high-quality education can have a positive impact on outcomes through:

  • understanding how a culture of inclusion and belonging enables good outcomes
  • strengthened awareness of driving high-quality effective teaching
  • inclusive routines and environments

Understanding and supporting children and young people with SEND

You'll learn how to understand and support differences in learning at a strategic level including through:

  • ensuring a consistent approach identifying needs
  • working with parents and families
  • supporting high-incidence barriers to learning

Additional support

You'll learn how to utilise different approaches to supporting learning barriers including through:

  • ensuring the use of effective targeted interventions by staff in your setting
  • effective deployment of teaching assistants and support staff
  • enabling positive transitions for pupils and learners

Implementation

You'll consider how to implement and drive change around inclusion at a setting level including through:

  • clear roles and responsibilities of staff
  • staffing models and use of enhanched provision
  • use of reasonable adjustments
  • capacity-building amongst your workforce including professional development
  • characteristics of succesful implementation
  • monitoring and review to assess impact

Course structure

The course includes 15 topics, outlined in the course framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

The course includes:

  • independent study
  • group coaching
  • virtual sessions with other participants

You’ll need to spend up to 1 hour a week working on this ETLT course.

If you can, you should do this during work hours in career development time.

Providers

You can train for this ETLT course with one of the following providers:

  • Ambition Institute
  • National Society for Education
  • Education Development Trust
  • LLSE
  • National Institute of Teaching
  • Teach First
  • University College London (UCL) Institute of Education

They work with delivery partners, such as teaching school hubs, to provide training.
