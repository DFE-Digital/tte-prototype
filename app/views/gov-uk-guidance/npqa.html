National Professional Qualification Advanced (NPQA)

Training courses for experienced education leaders looking to enhance their professional skills and leadership effectiveness.

About

NPQAs take around six months to complete and are grounded in evidence-based practice.

You do not need to have taken an NPQ to complete an NPQA.

Course features

  • duration: approximately 6 months, allowing for focused and manageable professional development
  • evidence-based content: informed by the latest research in education, leadership, and organisational improvement
  • flexible learning: blended learning includes online sessions, optional in-person sessions, and self-study
