ETLT courses

Leading SEND and inclusion (leaders) - learn about effective leadership and practice relating to inclusive school and college environments and supporting learners with SEND in your setting

Supporting SEND and inclusion (teachers) - earn about effective pedagogy and practice relating to inclusive teaching and learning environments and supporting learners with SEND

Teaching in reception course - learn about key areas of child development and how to apply this to teaching / classroom practice for reception year

ETLT providers

Providers are:

  • chosen by the Department for Education (DfE) in a fair and open procurement process
  • subject to quality assurance through Ofsted inspection

The individual ETLT course pages list the providers for each course.

All ETLT courses are underpinned by a framework that sets out what you should know and know how to do after completing an ETLT course.

ETLT training providers use these frameworks to design their courses.

Course structure

Courses last around 2-3 terms. The course structure, curriculum and method of delivery may vary between different providers.

During your course, you will:

  • follow an evidence-based curriculum, drawing on effective pedagogy, recent research and international best practice to give you the knowledge and skills to improve in your current role or take the next step in your career
  • take part in a mixture of live training and self-directed study
  • ETLT courses are developed in collaboration with the sector, informed by the best available research and evidence endorsed by the Education Endowment Foundation.

Funding

Funding to cover the ETLT course fees is available for teachers and school leaders in many state-funded settings in England, subject to eligibility criteria.

Eligibility for funding does not guarantee a place on a funded ETLT course.
