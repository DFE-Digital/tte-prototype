ETT courses

Teaching in reception course - learn about key areas of child development and how to apply this to teaching / classroom practice for reception year

ETT providers

Providers are:

  • chosen by the Department for Education (DfE) in a fair and open procurement process
  • subject to quality assurance through Ofsted inspection

The individual ETT course pages list the providers for each course.

All ETT courses are underpinned by a framework that sets out what you should know and know how to do after completing a ETT course.

ETT training providers use these frameworks to design their courses.

Course structure

Courses last around 2-3 terms. The course structure, curriculum and method of delivery may vary between different providers.

During your course, you will:

  • follow an evidence-based curriculum, drawing on effective pedagogy, recent research and international best practice to give you the knowledge and skills to improve in your current role or take the next step in your career
  • take part in a mixture of webinars and self-directed study
  • ETTs are developed in collaboration with the sector, informed by the best available research and evidence endorsed by the Education Endowment Foundation.

Funding

Funding to cover the course fees for ETTs is available for teachers and school leaders in many state-funded settings in England, subject to eligibility criteria.

Eligibility for funding does not guarantee a place on a funded ETT course.
