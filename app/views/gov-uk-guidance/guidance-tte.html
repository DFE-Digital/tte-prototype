What is an NPD course

National Professional Development courses are a Department for Education (DfE) initiative to provide the best evidence-based learning for experienced teachers and education leaders.

On successful completion of an NPD course, you will receive a certificate from DfE.

How long is an NPD course

Length of an NPD course will vary depending on the course you choose, and how your training provider delivers it.

Courses are a minimum of 14 hours and a maximum of 20 hours. It takes around two to three school terms to complete a course based on around one hour of learning a week.

You should check the individual course page for more details.

How the course is delivered

The course is delivered by DfE approved training providers only. It is taught using a mix of online learning, live sesssions (minimum 4 hours), and self-study.

Am I eligible for a free place on an NPD course

Funding is available for most state-funded education settings in England, including Academies. You can also self-fund, and this can be discussed with the training provider.

More information about eligibility for a free place can be found on the individual course pages.
