Guidance

Teaching in reception course

A course to help teachers learn about key areas of child development and how to put this knowledge into teaching practice to support good early education outcomes.

Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • teachers who currently or plan to work with reception age children
  • teachers who have already completed their induction

Overview

This two-term course will help you to:

  • develop your understanding of high-quality teaching practices specifically relevant to the reception year
  • understand strategies and approaches to support good development in key areas to enable children to achieve good outcomes

This course will:

  • take 2 terms to complete
  • involve up to 1 hour of study each week
  • be a mix of self-study and live training
  • start Autumn 2026

Funding

Funding to cover the course fees is available for most teachers and school leaders in state funded settings in England.

Eligibility

To apply for this course you should be teaching, or planning to teach, reception age children.

Course content

High-quality teaching and classroom practice

You'll learn how high-quality early education can have a positive impact on outcomes through:

  • strategies for effective teaching
  • enabling mastery of important foundational concepts
  • appropriate curriculum and formative assessment design
  • supporting positive transitions into and out of reception year

Supporting good development

You'll learn how to understand and support differences in child development in key areas to support access to learning, including through:

  • awareness of differences in development including SEND and disadvantage
  • enabling good communication and language
  • supporting physical development
  • encouraging personal, social and emotional, development
  • working with parents to support development goals

Course structure

The course includes 9 topics, outlined in the course framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

The course includes:

  • independent study
  • group coaching
  • virtual sessions with other participants

You’ll need to spend up to 1 hour a week working on this ETT course.

If you can, you should do this during work hours in career development time.

Providers

You can train for this ETT course with one of the following providers:

  • Ambition Institute
  • National Society for Education
  • Education Development Trust
  • LLSE
  • National Institute of Teaching
  • Teach First
  • University College London (UCL) Institute of Education

They work with delivery partners, such as teaching school hubs, to provide training.
