Guidance

Register for {{ data['reception-course-name'] }} {{ data['tte-programme-name-with-acronym'] }} course

Using the latest early years evidence, this course will help experienced teachers deepen their understanding of the key areas of child development. It will provide practical skills to help benchmark and enhance early education outcomes.

On successful completion of the course, you with be awarded a DfE accredited {{ data['tte-programme-name-acronym'] }} certificate.

Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • teachers who have completed their induction. Please note: there is no upper limit on years of experience
  • teachers who work with reception age children or are changing to a reception teaching role

Course overview

You'll learn how high-quality early education can have a positive imapct on outcomes through:

  • strategies for effective teaching
  • mastery of important foundational concepts
  • appropriate curriculum and assessment design
  • positive transitions for children moving in and out of reception year

During your course, you will:

  • follow an evidence-based curriculum, drawing on effective pedagogy, recent research and international best practice to give you the knowledge and skills to improve in your current role or take the next step in your career
  • take part in a mixture of live training and self-directed study
  • {{ data['tte-programme-name-acronym'] }} courses are developed in collaboration with the sector, informed by the best available research and evidence endorsed by the Education Endowment Foundation.

This course will:

  • take approximately 2 school terms to complete (minimum 20 hours)
  • be a mix of self-study and live online training
  • start in October 2026 or January and February 2027, depending on the training provider

Funding eligibility

Funding to cover the course fees is available for most teachers and leaders in state funded settings in England.

Eligibility

Funded places are available for most state-funded settings.

Examples of places that are not eligible for funding include hospital schools, Ministry of Defence (MoD) schools and schools outside of England.

If you are self-funding, you still need to register with the DfE.

When you register with the DfE, we will tell you if you are eligible for funding.
