Course summary

Empower your leadership to create inclusive, high‑quality special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) provisions

The SEND and Inclusion for Leaders NPD course is designed to support senior leaders, and aspiring leaders, to strengthen whole‑school approaches to SEND and inclusion.

Through expert‑led training, practical tools, and collaborative learning, this course helps leaders develop the confidence and knowledge to drive sustainable improvement for pupils with SEND.

15 hours of content

4 hours of live training

Optional in-person sessions across the country

Totally flexible study hours

Who is this course for?

This course is for:

  • Headteachers and senior education leaders
  • Deputy and assistant headteachers
  • Inclusion leads and SEND strategic leaders
  • Trust and local authority leaders with responsibility for SEND

Whether you are new to a leadership role or seeking to refine your existing SEND strategy, the course provides practical, transferable learning you can apply immediately.

What will I learn?

Participants will develop:

  • A deeper understanding of inclusive leadership and SEND systems relevant to the modern-day classroom
  • The skills to lead high‑quality SEND provision across your education settings
  • Confidence in strategic planning and evaluation for SEND
  • Stronger approaches to working with families, professionals and external agencies
  • Strategies to support staff development, wellbeing and accountability in your education setting

Course content overview

The course content is based on current policy, research and real‑world leadership practice.

Key themes include:

  • The national SEND landscape and inclusive leadership
  • Creating a clear vision and strategy for SEND
  • Effective use of reasonable adjustments
  • Supporting staff to deliver inclusive teaching and learning
  • Monitoring impact and improving outcomes for pupils with SEND
  • Building inclusive cultures within schools and trusts

How is the course delivered?

  • Fully funded places supported by the Department for Education (DfE)
  • Delivered through a blend of online sessions and self-study over 12 weeks (minimum 4 hours of live, interactive sessions). Commitment is one hour of study each week
  • Facilitated by experienced SEND and inclusion experts
  • Includes opportunities for peer collaboration and shared knowledge

Flexible, online and self-study is designed to fit alongside leadership responsibilities.

Benefits of completing this NPD course

By completing this course, leaders will:

  • Strengthen their impact on pupil outcomes and inclusion
  • Build confidence in leading complex SEND priorities
  • Develop a consistent, whole‑setting approach to inclusion
  • Gain recognised professional development through the NPD programme

Course details

  • Course title: SEND and Inclusion for Leaders – National Professional Development (NPD) course
  • Duration: Delivered over 12 weeks with one hours of online learning each week
  • Cost: Fully funded (subject to eligibility)
  • Certification: National Professional Development recognition from DfE
