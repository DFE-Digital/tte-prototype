Course summary

Empowering Teachers to Support Every Learner

Children are at a crucial stage in their development, and high-quality support can make all the difference- especially for those with special educational needs and disabilities (SEND). Our SEND and inclusion for teachers – National Professional Development (NPD) course, in partnership with Department for Education, uses the latest research to provide teachers and early years practitioners with up-to-date knowledge, confidence, and practical tools to create inclusive, nurturing, and high-impact learning environments.

15 hours of content

4 hours of live training

Optional in-person sessions across the country

Totally flexible study hours

Who this course is for

This course is designed for:

  • teachers who have completed their induction
  • experienced teachers who want to learn the latest methodologies

Whether you are newly qualified or have been teaching for many years, this course provides a strong foundation in evidence‑based practice.

Early assessment

  • Recognising developmental needs
  • Using observation and screening tools effectively
  • Working with parents and external professionals

How to create inclusive classroom environments

  • Adapting teaching strategies
  • Sensory-friendly classroom design
  • Supporting communication, emotional regulation, and behaviours

High‑quality teaching for SEND learners

  • Structuring routines and transitions
  • Strengthening language, literacy, and early maths foundations
