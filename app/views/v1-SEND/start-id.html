National professional development (NPD) courses Check funding eligibility and register for a course

Use this service to:

  • check eligibility for DfE funding, or tell us how you will self-fund
  • register with DfE for a NPD course
  • log in to see the status of your registration
  • change your training provider if you are already registered
  • get your certificate when you have completed the course

How to register

1. Choose a course

You can register for:

  • {{ data['send-course-name'] }}
  • {{ data['send-leader-course-name'] }}
  • {{ data['reception-course-name'] }}

2. Choose a training provider

All providers have been selected by the DfE. All providers deliver the same course content, however there may be differences in how they choose to deliver the course. You can visit their website to learn more.

{{ govukWarningText({ text: "You must register with DfE before you apply with a provider.", iconFallbackText: "Warning" }) }}
  • Acorn training
  • Cedar trust
  • Evergreen learning
  • Maple education
  • Redwood training
  • Willow network

3. Register with DfE

When you register with the DfE, we will:

  1. check and confirm your eligibility for funding
  2. notify your chosen training provider

Your provider will:

  1. contact you to complete the application
  2. confirm if they have a funded place available

If your training provider is unable to offer a funded place, you can come back and use this service to register with a different provider.

Before you start
