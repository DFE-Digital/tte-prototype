SEND and inclusion for leaders

National professional qualification advanced (NPQA) course

A course to help leaders develop their effective leadership and practice relating to inclusive school and college environments.

Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • head teachers
  • further education principals
  • SENCos in strategic roles
  • deputy-head teachers
  • senior leaders in education settings

Overview

This course is an NPQ advancedcourse.

This course will help you to:

  • develop your understanding of how inclusive practice drives improved outcomes at a whole-setting level, including attainment, engagement, and wellbeing
  • lead and sustain a culture of belonging and inclusion
  • strengthen your ability to design and implement systems that support pupils with SEND
  • ensure high-quality teaching and learning meets the needs of all pupils through a strategic, evidence-informed approach

This course will:

  • take approximately 3 school terms to complete (minimum 20 hours)
  • involve up to 1 hour of study each week
  • include a blend of self-study, facilitated training and optional in-person sessions
  • start in October 2027 and early 2028 (January or February), depending on the provider r

Funding eligibility

Funded places are available for most state-funded settings, including academy trusts.

Examples of places that are not eligible for funding include private schools and schools outside of England.

If you are self-funding, you still need to register with the DfE.

When you register with the DfE, we will tell you if you are eligible for funding.

Course content

You will explore how to lead a whole-setting culture of inclusion that improves outcomes, including:

  • embedding a shared vision of inclusion and belonging across staff
  • ensuring consistency in high-quality teaching
  • developing inclusive routines, systems, and environments

Understanding and supporting children and young people with SEND

You will develop your strategic leadership of SEND provision, including:

  • establishing robust and consistent processes
  • strengthening partnerships with parents, carers, and external professionals
  • addressing common barriers to learning at scale

Implementation

A key focus is on leading sustainable change, including:

  • defining clear roles, responsibilities, and accountability structures
  • designing effective staffing models
  • embedding reasonable adjustments as standard practice
  • building staff capability through professional development
  • Establishing systems to assess effectiveness and inform decision-making

Course structure

The course includes 15 topics, outlined in the course framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

If you can, you should do this during work hours in career development time.
