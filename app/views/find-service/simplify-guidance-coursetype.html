Find funded training for experienced teachers and education leaders

Get help to find Department for Education (DfE) funded training to develop your teaching practice and professional knowledge.

This service is for experienced teachers and education leaders who:

  • have completed their induction
  • work in any stage of education, from early years to further education
  • work in England

This service shows training courses that are:

  • designed by DfE and evidence-based
  • independently verified by the Education Endowment Foundation (EEF)
  • medium to long-term commitment (minimum 15 hours)
  • led by a qualified training facilitator (online, in-person, or both)

The service will search across the following:

  • National Professional Development (NPD) courses
  • National Professional Qualifications (NPQs)
  • National Professional Qualification Advanced (NPQA)

National Professional Development (NPD)

These courses are for experienced teachers who want to add depth to their knowledge in a specific subject area. They take less time than an NPQ (ETC)

Excellence in reception teaching - NPD

A course to help experienced reception teachers deepen their understanding of the key areas of child development.

Duration:
6 months
Length:
minimum 20 hours
Format:
Online. Optional in-person sessions
Award:
NPD Certificate

SEND and inclusion for teachers NPD

Develop your inclusive teaching practices and gain actionable skills to ensure the best learning outcomes for all pupils.

Duration:
6 months
Length:
minimum 20 hours
Format:
Online. Optional in-person sessions
Assessment type:
Based on attendance
Award:
NPD Certificate

Specialist National Professional Qualifications (NPQ)

Specialist NPQs can help you develop your classroom teaching or specialism.

Early years leadership NPQ

A course to help current or aspiring managers of nurseries develop expertise in leading early years education and staff and organisational management.

Duration:
18 months
Length:
1 to 2 hours of study each week
Format:
Mix of self-study and in-person group sessions
Assessment type:
Attendance and written assessment
Award:
National Professional Qualification (NPQ)

Leading teaching NPQ

A course to help teachers and middle educational leaders learn how to lead the teaching and learning of a subject, year group or phase

Duration:
12 months
Length:
1 to 2 hours of study each week
Format:
Mix of self-study and in-person group sessions
Assessment type:
Attendance and written assessment
Award:
National Professional Qualification (NPQ)

Leading behaviour and culture NPQ

12 months

1 to 2 hours of study each week

Assessment type:

Award:
National Professional Qualification (NPQ)
