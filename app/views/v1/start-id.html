How to register

To register, you first need to choose a training provider. All providers have been selected by the DfE and the course is endorsed by the Education Endowment Foundation.

1. Choose a training provider

All providers deliver the same course content, however there may be differences in how they choose to deliver the course. You can visit their website to learn more.

{{ govukWarningText({ text: "You must register with DfE before you apply with a provider.", iconFallbackText: "Warning" }) }}
  • Acorn training
  • Cedar trust
  • Evergreen learning
  • Maple education
  • Redwood training
  • Willow network

2. Register with DfE

When you register with the DfE, we will:

  1. check and confirm your eligibility for funding
  2. notify your chosen training provider

Your provider will:

  1. contact you to complete the application
  2. confirm if they have a funded place available

If your training provider is unable to offer a funded place, you can come back and use this service to register with a different provider.

Before you start
