How to register

1. Choose a course

You can register for:

  • {{ data['send-leader-course-name'] }} NPQA
  • {{ data['send-course-name'] }} NPD
  • {{ data['reception-course-name'] }} NPD

2. Choose a start date

The courses usually start in October and February each year. Some providers do not deliver every start date.

3. Choose a training provider

All providers have been selected by the DfE. All providers deliver the same course content, however there may be differences in how they choose to deliver the course.

Go to an individual course page to see the relevant training providers.

4. Register with DfE

To register with DfE you will need:

  • an ID verified GOV One Login account (you will need a passport or driving license and mobile phone to do this)
  • to connect your One Login account to your teaching record (you will need your National Insurance number and Teaching Record Number)

You will be prompted to do these during the registration process.

When you register with the DfE, we will:

  • confirm your eligibility for funding
  • notify your chosen training provider
  • email you a unique link to complete your training provider's application

If your training provider is unable to offer a funded place, you can come back and use this service to register with a different provider.
