{{ data['tte-combined-name'] }} Check funding eligibility to register for a professional development course

To register for a course, you first need to:

  • check your eligibility for funding or tell us you will self-fund

After we have checked your eligibility, you can continue to register. We will use your eligibility result as part of your registration.

To complete your registration, you will need to:

  1. log in using Gov One Login – you can create an account if you do not already have one.
    • you may need your passport to prove your identity
    • you’ll need a mobile to receive a verification code
  2. answer some questions to complete your registration
    • choose your course start date – courses usually start in either October or January but not all providers will offer all dates
    • choose a training provider
  3. submit your registration

By continuing, you agree to our privacy notice.
