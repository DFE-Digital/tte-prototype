Excellence in reception teaching uses the latest evidence from early years education to help teachers strengthen their practice and deepen their understanding of child development.

NPD courses are endorsed by the Education Endowment Foundation.

On successful completion of the course, participants will be awarded a Department for Education (DfE) accredited NPD certificate.

Who this course is for

This course is suitable for:

  • teachers who have completed their induction
  • experienced reception teachers
  • teachers moving into a reception role

This course is not designed for teaching assistants.

About the course

This course is part of the National Professional Development (NPD) suite of courses. It was developed to support reception teachers in delivering high-quality teaching and learning in the reception year.

The course helps teachers build the knowledge, skills and professional behaviours needed to support all children to achieve and thrive.

You'll develop practical approaches that you can apply in your classroom immediately.

Why do teachers need this course

Reception teachers play a vital role in shaping children’s early educational journeys.

In the Every child achieving and thriving white paper, we committed to new training courses for teachers.

In the Giving every child the best start in life strategy, we committed to developing a new training course for reception class teachers.

This course will help teachers deliver high-quality reception teaching by providing the knowledge, tools and confidence to apply new learning approaches.

What you will learn

You will learn practical strategies to improve children’s outcomes, including how to:

  • apply evidence-based approaches in the classroom
  • establish effective communication and language skills to support children’s learning
  • build expertise in identifying and adapting to barriers to learning, including special educational needs and disabilities (SEND)
  • use physical activity to support children’s well-being and motor-skills
  • plan an effective appropriate curriculum and assessments
  • support smooth transitions for children into and out of reception

Read the full content framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

Providers

You can choose from one of the following DfE-approved providers:

  • Acorn training
  • Cedar trust
  • Evergreen learning
  • Maple education
  • Redwood training
  • Willow network

How the course is delivered

The course is designed to fit around your teaching commitments. Where possible, you should complete the course during your professional development time.

This course will:

  • take approximately two school terms to complete (minimum 20 hours)
  • include a mix of self-study and live online training
  • start in October and January, depending on the training provider

Funding

Funded places are available for most state-funded settings, including academy trusts.

Examples of places that are not eligible for funding include private schools and schools outside of England.

We will check your eligibility for funding before you register for the course.
