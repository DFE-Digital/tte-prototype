Who this course is for

This course is for:

  • teachers who want to develop their knowledge and skills in inclusive practice, including supporting children and young people with SEND

About the course

This course is from the {{ data['tte-programme-name-with-acronym'] }} suite of courses.

This course will help you to:

  • develop your understanding of high-quality teaching practices and inclusive approaches to supporting learning
  • understand evidence-based approaches to support children and young people with SEND

What you will learn

Inclusive practice

You'll learn how high-quality education can have a positive impact on outcomes through:

  • understanding how a culture of inclusion and belonging enables good outcomes
  • understanding the features of high-quality teaching
  • creating inclusive routines and environments

Understanding and supporting children and young people with SEND

You'll learn how to develop an adaptive teaching approach by:

  • identifying needs and barriers to learning
  • understanding differences in learning
  • working with parents and families

Additional support

You'll learn how to utilise different approaches to supporting learning barriers, including through:

  • using effective targeted interventions
  • working with teaching assistants and support staff
  • enabling positive transitions for pupils and learners

Providers

Courses usually start in either October or January, but some providers may not offer all dates.

You can train for this {{ data['tte-programme-name-acronym'] }} course with one of the following providers.

  • Acorn training
  • Cedar trust
  • Evergreen learning
  • Maple education
  • Redwood training
  • Willow network

How the course is delivered

The course includes 9 topics, outlined in the course framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

Where possible, you should complete the course during your allocated professional development time.

This course will:

  • take approximately two school terms to complete (minimum 20 hours)
  • include a mix of self-study and live online training
  • start in October or January, depending on the training provider

Funding

Funded places are available for most state-funded settings, including academy trusts.

Examples of places that are not eligible for funding include schools outside of England, excluding Ministry of Defence overseas schools.

We will check your eligibility for funding before you register for the course.
