Find training for experienced teachers and education leaders

Find Department for Education (DfE) funded training to develop your teaching practice and professional knowledge.

This service is for experienced teachers and education leaders who:

  • have completed their induction
  • work in any stage of education, from early years to further education
  • work in England

This service shows training courses that are:

  • designed by DfE and evidence-based
  • independently verified by the Education Endowment Foundation (EEF)
  • medium to long-term commitment (minimum 15 hours)
  • led by a training facilitator (online, in-person, or both)

The service includes the following:

  • National Professional Development (NPD) courses
  • National Professional Qualifications (NPQs)
  • National Professional Qualification Advanced (NPQA)

National Professional Development (NPD)

These short courses are for experienced teachers who want to add depth to their knowledge in a specific subject area. They run for around 6 months and are online with optional in-person sessions. Completion is based on attendance.

Specialist National Professional Qualifications (NPQ)

Specialist NPQs can help you develop your classroom teaching or specialism. They run for around 12 months and are a mix of online and in-person sessions. There is an assessment at the end of the course, after passing you will be awarded with the qualification.

Leadership NPQs

Leadership NPQs can help you develop your leadership skills or progress to a leadership role. They run for around 18 months and are a mix of online and in-person sessions. There is an assessment at the end of the course, after passing you will be awarded with the qualification.

NPQ Advanced

Courses for experienced leaders. These are aimed at people who have previously completed an NPQ. They run for around 9 months and are online with optional in-person sessions. Completion is based on attendance.
