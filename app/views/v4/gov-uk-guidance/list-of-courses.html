Find Department for Education (DfE) funded training for experienced teachers and leaders

This training helps experienced teachers and leaders working in early years, schools and further education (FE) to develop their teaching practice, leadership skills and specialist knowledge.

From: Department for Education

Published: 3 March 2027

These training courses are for people who have completed their induction. The courses are:

  • designed by DfE and evidence based
  • independently verified by the Education Endowment Foundation (EEF)
  • a medium to long-term commitment (minimum 15 hours)
  • led by a training facilitator (online, in-person and both)

National Professional Qualification (NPQ)

Nationally recognised qualifications for teachers and leaders looking to develop leadership expertise.

Specialist NPQs

kfdjhs

Leading teacher development NPQ

Help teachers in your setting develop their skills.

Leading teaching NPQ

Lead the teaching and learning of a subject, year group or phase.

Leading behaviour and culture NPQ

Promote a culture of good behaviour and high expectations.

Leading literacy NPQ

Promote literacy across a whole educational setting, year group, key stage or phase.

Leading primary mathematics NPQ

Use mastery approaches to lead maths teaching in your school.

Leadership NPQs

Special educational needs co-ordinator's (SENCO) NPQ

Senior leadership NPQ

Develop leadership expertise to improve outcomes for teachers and pupils.

Headship NPQ

Become an expert school leader and outstanding headteacher.

Executive leadership NPQ

Develop the expertise to run large educational organisations and lead change and improvement.

Early years leadership NPQ

Manage your staff and organisation to provide high-quality early years education and care.

Early headship coaching offer
