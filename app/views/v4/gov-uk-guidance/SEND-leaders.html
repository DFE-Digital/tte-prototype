Who this course is for

This course is for leaders responsible for improving inclusive practice across their setting, including:

  • school leaders
  • senior leaders in further education (FE)
  • leaders in education trusts

About the course

This course is a National Professional Qualification Advanced (NPQA) course. It is shorter than an NPQ and is designed to be completed after an NPQ, although not essential.

On successful completion, you'll receive a DfE accredited certificate.

This course will help you to:

  • lead a culture of inclusion and belonging across your setting
  • improve outcomes for children and young people with SEND
  • develop effective whole-setting approaches to identifying and supporting additional needs
  • implement sustainable changes that improve inclusive practice

Course content

Inclusive culture and environments

You'll learn how high-quality education can have a positive impact on outcomes by:

  • building a culture of inclusion and belonging
  • understanding how high-quality teaching supports inclusion and positive outcomes
  • creating inclusive routines and environments

Understanding and supporting children and young people with SEND

You'll learn how to understand and support differences in learning at a strategic level including by:

  • developing consistent approaches to identifying additional needs
  • working with parents and families
  • supporting common barriers to learning

Approaches to learning barriers

You'll learn how to use different approaches to learning barriers including through:

  • ensuring the use of effective targeted interventions by staff in your setting
  • effective deployment of teaching assistants and support staff
  • enabling positive transitions for pupils and learners

Implementation

You'll consider how to implement and drive change around inclusion at a setting level including through:

  • clear roles and responsibilities of staff
  • staffing models and use of enhanced provision
  • use of reasonable adjustments
  • building staff expertise through professional development
  • characteristics of successful implementation
  • monitoring and review to assess impact

Providers

Courses usually start in either October or January, but some providers may not offer all dates.

You can train for this NPQA course with one of the following providers.

  • Acorn training
  • Cedar trust
  • Evergreen learning
  • Maple education
  • Redwood training
  • Willow network

How the course is delivered

The course includes 15 topics, outlined in the course framework. Providers use this framework to design their course.

Where possible, you should complete the course during your professional development time.

This course will:

  • take approximately three school terms to complete (minimum 20 hours)
  • include a combination of self-study and live online training with optional in-person sessions
  • start in October or January, depending on the training provider

Funding

Funded places are available for most state-funded settings, including academy trusts.

Examples of places that are not eligible for funding include schools outside of England, excluding Ministry of Defence overseas schools.

We will check your eligibility for funding before you register for the course.
