How to register

After you have signed in, you'll be asked to:

  • select your start date and provider
  • submit your registration

Your provider will then contact you to complete your application.

How to sign in

To sign in, you'll need a GOV.UK One Login account. You'll be asked to create one if you don't already have one.

You'll need:

  • an email address - we recommend you use your personal email so that you don’t lose access to your account
  • your Teacher Record Number (TRN). If you do not have a TRN we’ll give you one after you’ve registered. If you’ve lost your TRN use the find a lost TRN service

To verify your identity through GOV.UK One Login, most people will need:

  • a passport or driving licence
  • a mobile phone to receive a verification code

You will only need to verify your identity once for all services you use One Login for.
